Advantages of Practice :


  1. Improves and reinforces skills.
  2. Increases automaticity and frees up mental resources.
  3. Builds confidence.
  4. Enables error correction and adaptation.
  5. Develops resilience and motivation.

Disadvantages of Practice


  • Reinforces bad habits if not done mindfully.
  • Leads to burnout and fatigue.
  • Causes diminishing returns.
  • Hinders creativity and adaptability.
  • Causes mental and emotional stress.
